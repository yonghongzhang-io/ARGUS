\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The causal effect of a climate policy is unobservable; the
\emph{identification credibility} of the study estimating it is a tractable
target for AI assistance. \sys{} operationalizes it: a bounded pipeline turns
identification into auditable dimensions and evidence-grounded risk reports;
flaw injection supplies reproducible local ground truth without the true
effect; and a human-gold diagnostic pilot shows expert labels can both evaluate
the auditor and yield rules that reduce its systematic over-severity in-sample.
Future work: expert-authored flaws, expert annotation at scale (the central
open challenge), reviewer-assistance studies, and settings beyond
one synthetic fixture. Auditing identification credibility is a tractable and
responsible role for AI in environmental-policy research.
