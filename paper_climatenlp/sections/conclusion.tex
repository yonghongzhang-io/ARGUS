\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The causal effect of a climate policy is unobservable; whether a study's
reported evidence supports its identification assumptions can be assessed.
\sys{} operationalizes that assessment: a bounded pipeline turns
identification into auditable dimensions and evidence-grounded risk reports;
flaw injection supplies local ground truth without the true effect; and an
adjudicated pilot shows that human labels can both evaluate the auditor and
yield rules that reduce its systematic over-severity in-sample. \sys{} links
dimension-level assessments to retrieved passages, while the pilot exposes
substantial abstention and over-severity; its value in expert review and on
climate-policy papers is untested.
