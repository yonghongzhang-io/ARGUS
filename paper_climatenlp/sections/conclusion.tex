\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The causal effect of a climate policy is unobservable; whether a study's
reported evidence supports its identification assumptions can be assessed.
\sys{} operationalizes that assessment with a bounded, evidence-grounded
pipeline, flaw injection as local ground truth, and a reconciled pilot whose human labels evaluate the auditor and score a
pre-specified rule that reduces its over-severity in-sample; its value
in expert review and on climate-policy papers is untested.
