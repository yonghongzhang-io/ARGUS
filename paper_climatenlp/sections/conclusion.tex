\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The causal effect of a climate policy is unobservable; whether a study's
reported evidence supports its identification assumptions can be assessed.
\sys{} operationalizes that assessment with a bounded pipeline that turns
identification into auditable, evidence-grounded dimensions, flaw injection as
local ground truth, and an adjudicated pilot whose human labels both evaluate
the auditor and yield rules that reduce its over-severity in-sample; its value
in expert review and on climate-policy papers is untested.
