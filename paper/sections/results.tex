\section{Preliminary Results}
\label{sec:results}

We run the deterministic keyword baseline over all 11 flaws on two parsed-paper
fixtures (a generic supported DID study and a China carbon-ETS pilot fixture).
Table~\ref{tab:baseline} reports the headline metrics; full per-flaw results are
in \texttt{experiments/phase1\_pilot/results.md}.

\paragraph{Reading the result.}
The baseline never false-alarms, but detection is low and concentrated on
omission flaws whose vocabulary is not echoed elsewhere; it is systematically
blind to commission flaws where flawed-but-plausible evidence is present. The
failure mode is informative: keyword presence is not evidence \emph{adequacy}.
This is the gap a reasoning-based auditor must close, and it frames the
remainder of the doctoral programme (\S\ref{sec:discussion}).
