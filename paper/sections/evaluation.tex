\section{Evaluation by Flaw Injection}
\label{sec:evaluation}

Because the true causal effect is unobserved, we obtain \emph{local} ground truth
by perturbation. Starting from a credible paper, we inject exactly one known flaw
targeting a single identification dimension (flaw taxonomy:
\texttt{config/flaw\_taxonomy.yaml}) and audit the clean and injected versions.
We report \emph{detection} (was the injected flaw caught?), \emph{false alarm}
(did the clean version stay quiet?), and \emph{localization} (was the right
dimension flagged?).

\paragraph{Realistic perturbation, not sentinels.}
Injection performs a structural edit---\emph{removing} the supporting
section/table for an omission-type threat, or \emph{replacing} a section with
flawed-but-natural prose for a commission-type threat. Critically, the
replacement does \emph{not} contain the phrases the detector looks for. An
earlier design that inserted sentinel sentences naming the flaw made detection
circular (a trivial $1.000$); the realistic design removes that leakage and is
enforced by a regression test.
