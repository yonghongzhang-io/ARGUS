\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

ARGUS reframes AI assistance for empirical policy research as \emph{auditing
identification credibility}, not adjudicating causal truth. A bounded-agentic
pipeline, an auditable rubric, and a flaw-injection methodology give a
reproducible foundation; an honest baseline shows that retrieval alone is
insufficient, motivating reasoning over evidence adequacy as the core of the
doctoral programme.
