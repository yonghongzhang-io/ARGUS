\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{DID identification and its threats.}
Recent econometrics has catalogued how DID fails under heterogeneous, staggered
timing \cite{goodmanbacon2021,sunabraham2021,callaway2021} and how pre-trend
tests can mislead \cite{roth2023}. ARGUS operationalizes this literature as an
auditable rubric (\S\ref{sec:framework}) rather than a new estimator.
