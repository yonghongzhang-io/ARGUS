\section{Discussion and Research Plan}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Threats to validity.}
Results use synthetic fixtures ($N$ small); the baseline is a deliberate lower
bound. Local ground truth certifies \emph{injected} threats, not the absence of
others.

\paragraph{Planned work.}
(1)~A reasoning-based extraction/assessment policy that judges evidence
\emph{adequacy}, evaluated against the baseline of \S\ref{sec:results};
(2)~a corpus of real DID environmental-policy papers with expert annotations;
(3)~human-expert studies on report usefulness and trust;
(4)~broader policy domains beyond the China ETS case.
