# Research Questions

**RQ1 — Decomposability.** Can the causal-identification credibility of a
difference-in-differences (DID) environmental-policy study be decomposed into a
fixed set of *auditable dimensions*, each phrased as an
assumption → testable-implication → in-paper-evidence chain?

**RQ2 — Auditability without the counterfactual.** Can an AI system assess each
dimension's evidence and *localize* identification risk, given that the true
causal effect (the counterfactual) is never observed?

**RQ3 — Validation via flaw injection.** Does synthetic flaw injection provide
*reproducible local ground truth* — detection, false-alarm, localization — for
evaluating such an auditor without requiring the true effect?

**RQ4 — The limits of retrieval.** Where does keyword/retrieval-based auditing
fail, and what does that reveal about the need to reason over the *adequacy* of
evidence rather than its mere presence?
